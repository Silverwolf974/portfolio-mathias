Forecasting bike availability in Oslo

How many bikes will be left at a station in an hour? I worked on that forecast using station records and weather observations.

My contribution

I matched observations by station and time to compare bike availability across different times of day and weather conditions.

What I learned

On the test data, predictions differed from the actual count by 3.02 bikes on average. I learned to interpret that figure in practical terms: at a nearly empty station, three bikes can change the decision to send more.
