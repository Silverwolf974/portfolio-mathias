Finding names in text

Evaluation of how well a model trained on Wikipedia identifies people, companies and products in tweets.

SchoolINSA Lyon
DomainText analysis

Context and objective

Find out whether a tool that works on edited articles remains reliable on short, informal messages.

My contribution

I got the original code running again and checked how its answers were scored. One test gave a perfect score even when the system linked the wrong names. I then ran the final experiments on Google Colab.

Results

For company names, the recognition score fell from 0.68 on Wikipedia to 0.07 on tweets.

What I learned

I learned to inspect examples before drawing conclusions: some errors came from the model, while others came from ambiguity in the expected answers.
