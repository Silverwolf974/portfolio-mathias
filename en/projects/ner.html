Finding names in text

I tested whether an AI model trained on Wikipedia could also identify people, companies and products in tweets.

My contribution

I got the original code running again and checked how its answers were scored. One test gave a perfect score even when the system linked the wrong names. I then ran the final experiments on Google Colab.

What I learned

The model was much less reliable at identifying companies in tweets than on Wikipedia. I learned to inspect examples before drawing conclusions: some errors came from the model, while others came from ambiguity in the expected answers.

Tools
