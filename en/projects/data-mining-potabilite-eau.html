Water potability classification

Building and interpreting a binary classification model using logistic regression on a real water-quality dataset.

TypeData Mining lab (ESIROI)
TeamTwo students
DataWater Quality (Kaggle), approximately 6,400 samples
StatusCompleted

Context

The aim was to build a binary logistic regression model, evaluate its performance and explore optimisation strategies. The Kaggle Water Quality dataset contains chemical and biological measurements of water samples, with a potable/non-potable target variable.

Work completed

  • Cleaning: converting invalid values to NaN, handling missing data and checking data types.
  • Exploratory analysis: distributions, correlations, boxplots, outliers and pairplots.
  • Feature engineering: aggregate variables, such as total heavy-metal concentration.
  • Standardisation and multicollinearity analysis (VIF).
  • Logistic regression and interpretation of coefficients and p-values (statsmodels).

Results

The model converged with a pseudo-R² of around 0.30, identifying significant variables including aluminium, arsenic, cadmium and perchlorate. The substantial class imbalance was taken into account when interpreting the results.

Skills demonstrated

Careful preparation of imperfect real-world data, exploratory analysis and statistical interpretation of a model beyond simply training it.
